Product Vision

Kurukshetra Saarthi is an AI-powered travel companion that helpsvisitors explore the spiritual, historical and cultural heritage ofKurukshetra.

The experience should feel luxurious, calm, timeless and trustworthy.

Inspiration: - Apple Human Interface Guidelines - Airbnb - NationalGeographic - Aman Resorts - Incredible India (Premium)

The UI must never resemble a government portal.

Color Philosophy

Every color has meaning.

Royal Saffron represents the sunrise over Kurukshetra and is the brandcolor.

Temple Gold represents temples, brass ornaments and sacred heritage.

Warm Cream represents handmade manuscript paper.

Ancient Maroon represents the Mahabharata and traditional architecture.

Peacock Indigo represents guidance, maps, navigation and Krishna'swisdom. It is not the primary brand color; it is reserved forinteraction and navigation.

Color Usage Rules

Royal Saffron: - Primary buttons - Active bottom navigation - PrimaryCTA - Hero actions - Progress indicators

Temple Gold: - Decorative rules - Ratings - Borders - Highlights -Premium badges

Ancient Maroon: - Large headings - Dark sections - Footer

Peacock Indigo: - Maps - Current location - Routes - AI assistant -Links - Informational chips

Never use Peacock Indigo as the dominant visual color of a screen.
